Privacy Policy

App: Infinity IPTV (LG webOS TV) · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Summary

Infinity IPTV is a media player. It does not provide, host or sell any TV channels, movies or series. It only plays content from playlists that you add yourself. The app has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Data stored on your TV

  • Playlists you add: name, server URL, username and password (Xtream Codes) or the M3U link.
  • Your favorites, recently watched channels, watch progress and app settings.

This data is kept only in the app's local storage on your TV. It is never sent to us. Deleting a playlist in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Network connections

  • The app connects only to the servers and links in the playlists you add, to load channel lists, program guides, images and video streams. Those services are run by your provider, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • If you use the optional "add from phone" (QR code) feature, the playlist details you enter on your phone are encrypted on the phone and passed to your TV through a public relay service (ntfy.sh). The relay sees only encrypted data that it cannot read, and it deletes the message automatically (within about 12 hours).

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not track which channels or videos you watch.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising IDs or analytics.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
