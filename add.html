Scan the QR code

Open Add New Playlist in the Infinity IPTV app on your TV and scan the QR code shown there with your phone camera.

Add New Playlist

Enter the details from your IPTV provider and press Send to TV. Your TV adds the playlist automatically.

Sending…

Your details are encrypted on this phone before they are sent, and only your TV can read them.
Infinity IPTV is a media player and does not provide any content.
